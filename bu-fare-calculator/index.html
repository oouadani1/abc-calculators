        Where we got these numbers
        • Fares and rates are from mbta.com and BU Transportation Services, checked 2026-10-01. Treat this as a helpful estimate, not a guarantee.
        • BU covers a fixed 50% of the pass cost through Edenred for all regular faculty and staff. That rate isn't adjustable here because it isn't adjustable in BU's actual benefit.
        • One-way trips per week can model any pattern (weekend-only, midday-only, more than 5 days), not just a standard weekday round trip.
        • Monthly totals convert a weekly number using 4.33 weeks per month (52 weeks a year ÷ 12). Pay-Per-Ride's total is your one-way fare × trips per week × 4.33; Monthly Pass is simply MBTA's current sticker price. Reduced Fare and the Commuter Rail promo each use their own price schedule, broken out as their own line.
        • Driving estimates use BU's published Charles River FLEX Permit and Medical Campus garage rates. Charles River Zone 1's weekend rate is lower ($10/day vs. $14); this tool uses the weekday rate. Crosstown Garage's daily rate is post-tax; both Charles River zones and the monthly permits are pre-tax.
        • "Will you drive and park at this station?" uses that station's MBTA weekday parking rate (the largest lot when there's more than one) × the number of days a week you enter, and applies BU's 50% subsidy the same way it applies to your pass. Only stations with MBTA parking show this option.
        • Subsidizing more than one Commuter Rail zone? Each row lists that zone's price × headcount; those add up to Full monthly cost below them.
        • Every dollar amount on this page is rounded to the nearest whole dollar for readability. The math underneath is exact; rounding only changes what's displayed, not what's calculated.
