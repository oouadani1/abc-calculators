            Where we got these numbers
            • Fares and rates are from mbta.com and BU Transportation Services, checked 2026-10-01. Treat this as a helpful estimate, not a guarantee.
            • BU covers a fixed 50% of the pass cost through Edenred for all regular faculty and staff. That rate isn't adjustable here because it isn't adjustable in BU's actual benefit. BU's subsidy, and the pre-tax savings itemized below it, only apply to the Monthly Pass; BU does not subsidize single-ride fares, so Pay-Per-Ride always shows the full MBTA price.
            • Because Monthly Pass deductions run through payroll pre-tax, "Your estimated monthly cost" also nets out an estimated 30% in tax savings on top of BU's subsidy. The exact amount depends on your federal tax bracket (10-37%), plus Massachusetts state tax (5.0%), FICA (6.2%), and PFML (0.5%), for a combined marginal rate of at least 22.7%. This tool uses 30% as a representative average; your actual savings may differ. The same 30% applies to Personal Car's pre-tax parking options, and to whichever pass the Drive comparison card is matched against.
            • Round-trips per week can model any pattern (weekend-only, midday-only, more than 5 round-trips, an odd one-way errand counted as its own round-trip), not just a standard weekday commute.
            • Monthly totals convert a weekly number using 4.33 weeks per month (52 weeks a year ÷ 12). Pay-Per-Ride's total is your one-way fare × (round-trips × 2 one-way rides) × 4.33; Monthly Pass is simply MBTA's current sticker price. Reduced Fare and the Commuter Rail promo each use their own price schedule, broken out as their own line.
            • Commuter Rail Pay-Per-Ride also adds a one-way Subway & Bus fare per trip, since a CR Monthly Pass includes unlimited subway/bus system-wide but paying per ride on CR doesn't. The only exception is the Framingham/Worcester Line (Worcester through Lansdowne/Boston Landing), whose riders can reach Charles River Campus directly with no transfer. This assumes a Charles River Campus destination; Medical Campus commuters on that line would still need a transfer that isn't modeled here.
            • Driving estimates use BU's published Charles River FLEX Permit and Medical Campus garage rates. Charles River Zone 1's weekend rate is lower ($10/day vs. $14); this tool uses the weekday rate. Crosstown Garage's daily rate is post-tax; both Charles River zones and the monthly permits are pre-tax.
            • "Will you drive to and park at this station?" (Commuter Rail) and "Will you drive to and park at a T station?" (Subway & Bus) both use that station's MBTA weekday parking rate (the largest lot when there's more than one) × the number of days a week you enter. BU offers an additional 50% parking subsidy if you already have any BU-subsidized Monthly Pass, not just Commuter Rail, so Pay-Per-Ride shows the full, unsubsidized parking cost either way. Only stations with MBTA parking are in these lists.
            • Ferry one-way and monthly prices are confirmed from mbta.com/fares/ferry-fares. Winthrop/Quincy and Lynn's Reduced Fare prices match the Commuter Rail zones they're fare-integrated with (Zone 1 and Zone 2); Hingham/Hull's Reduced Fare monthly price has no official published figure, so it's estimated using the same ~50%-of-regular ratio the other routes follow.
            • Bluebikes membership, if added, is MBTA-independent: BU's discounted $75.50/year rate ($6.29/month) is simply added on top of whichever card you're comparing, unaffected by BU's transit subsidy or pre-tax savings.
            • Every dollar amount on this page is rounded to the nearest whole dollar for readability. The math underneath is exact; rounding only changes what's displayed, not what's calculated.
