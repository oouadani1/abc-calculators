Where we got these numbers
  • Fares are from mbta.com, checked 2026-07-10, and change over time. Treat this as a helpful estimate, not a guarantee.
  • Picking a number of days assumes a round trip each day: one ride there, one ride back.
  • Pay-Per-Ride's total monthly cost is your one-way fare × 2 for the round trip × the number of days you picked × 4.345, the average number of weeks in a month. LinkPass is $2.40 a ride. Regional Rail ranges from $2.40 to $13.25 a ride depending on your zone.
  • Monthly Pass's total monthly cost is simply the current MBTA price for that pass: $90 for LinkPass, or $90 to $426 for Regional Rail depending on your zone.
  • Employer subsidy and pre-tax savings are applied the same way to both options.
  • The 30% pre-tax savings default is MBTA's own Perq page figure, not an exact number. Adjust it if you know your own tax rate.
