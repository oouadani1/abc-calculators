Where we got these numbers
  • Fares are from mbta.com, checked 2026-08-19, and change over time. Treat this as a helpful estimate, not a guarantee.
  • Picking a number of days assumes a round trip each day: one ride there, one ride back.
  • Pay-Per-Ride's total monthly cost is your one-way fare × 2 for the round trip × the number of days you picked × 4.345, the average number of weeks in a month. LinkPass is $2.40 a ride. Commuter Rail ranges from $2.40 to $13.25 a ride depending on your zone.
  • Monthly Pass's total monthly cost (Full monthly cost on the employer side) is simply the current MBTA price for that pass: $90 for LinkPass, or $90 to $426 for Commuter Rail depending on your zone, before any promo or contribution.
  • Employer subsidy and pre-tax savings are applied the same way to both options.
  • If you're subsidizing more than one Commuter Rail zone, Passes by zone lists each zone's full sticker price times headcount, and those subtotals add up to Full monthly cost right below them. The promo and your contribution are applied once, after that, to reach Your estimated monthly cost.
  • The 30% pre-tax savings default is MBTA's own Perq page figure, not an exact number. Adjust it if you know your own tax rate.
