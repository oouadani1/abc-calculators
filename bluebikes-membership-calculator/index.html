Where we got these numbers
  • Gold and Silver pricing is from bluebikes.com, checked 2026-08-25, and change over time. Treat this as a helpful estimate, not a guarantee.
  • Gold is the corporate group rate of $101.50 per person, per year, fully covered by your organization.
  • Silver splits that same $101.50 group rate evenly: $50.75 from your organization, $50.75 from each employee.
  • Savings compare against Bluebikes' standard individual Annual Membership ($133.50/year), what someone would pay without a group rate at all.
  • Bulk pricing on short-term and monthly passes is a separate Bluebikes program, not a discount on top of Gold or Silver, see Bluebikes' bulk pricing for details.
